\section{Simulation}

\subsection{Noise and PSF}

Our simulation is divided into $2500$ sub-samples. The noise property and PSF are the same for galaxies in
one sub-sample while they vary among different sub-samples. We randomly sample $2500$ noise variances and 
PSF models from the coadd images of HSC-WIDE layer within the footprint of the HSC shape catalog. The PSF 
models are reconstructed by the HSC pipeline.

Noise on the coadd images has spacial correlation due to the warping process to resample single exposures on 
the common pixel frame. We sample the noise correlations from the blank pixels on the coadd images. The sampled
correlations, which are noisy at individual level, are subsequently divided into eight groups and stacked in 
each group to create eight different noise correlations with high signal to noise ratio.

Each sub-sample is assigned with a noise PSF model, noise variance and noise correlation function. We then reweight
each subsample to match the seeing histograms and noise variance histograms of the simulation to the observation. 
To note, the HSC WIDE layer is divided six observational fields (XMM, GAMA09H, WIDE12H, GAMA15H, VVDS, HECTOMAP) 
and the reweighting is conducted for each field. The seeing histograms and noise variance histograms of the observation
and simulation are shown in Figure \ref{fig:noiHist} and Figure \ref{fig:fwhmHist}.

\subsection{Galaxies}
We use the galaxy training sample created by \citet{HSC1-GREAT3Sim} as our input galaxies. \citet{HSC1-GREAT3Sim} 
selects galaxies from the HSC WIDE-DEPTH layer \citep{HSC1-data} with CModel magnitude less than $25.2$. 
Subsequently, \citet{HSC1-GREAT3Sim} finds the centers of these selected galaxies on the COSMOS-HST 
\citep{HST-ACSpipe} exposures and cuts out postage-stamps around these positions. These positions are set 
to as the centers of the postage-stamps and the postage sizes are required to be larger than $10.75$ arcsec 
to contain the galaxy light and structures of neighboring sources. 

We use Galsim \citep{Galsim}, which is an open-source package for galaxy image simulation to simulate the HSC-like 
images. The PSF effect is removed from each input postage-stamp by deconvolving the COSMOS HST PSF model then the
postage-stamp is convolved with an assigned HSC PSF model. The original noise is whitened for each postage stamp 
then a noise with an assigned HSC variance and an assigned HSC nosie correlation function is added to the postage-stamp.
